🏝️ Castaway Co-op

Two survivors. One island. Gather, build, hunt, fish, and escape.

Coming back? Use the same code and your original name. You continue exactly where you left off.

How to play
  • Move WASD / arrows, or the on-screen joystick. Look drag the screen. Zoom wheel / pinch.
  • E or the big button: chop, mine, pick, hunt, drink, cook, sleep.
  • B craft tools and build · I bag & eat · F fish (face the sea) · Enter chat · Shift sprint.
  • Keep hunger, thirst, energy and warmth up. Nights are cold and wolves roam, so build a fire and a shelter.
  • Win by building the escape raft ⛵ or surviving 30 days.
